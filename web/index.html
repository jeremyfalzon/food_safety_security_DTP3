    Method

    How the model was built

    Data

    Indicators come from the World Bank DataBank and the FAO's cost of a healthy diet and food security datasets. The sample covers 39 countries with no missing values in the modelled fields.

    Model

    A multiple linear regression fitted with batch gradient descent (1,500 iterations, learning rate 0.01) on z-scored features. The target is the prevalence of undernourishment, in percent of the population.

    Evaluation

    A 70/30 train–test split, repeated from the original notebook with seed 9022. Adjusted R² and RMSE are reported on the holdout countries, not on the data used to fit the coefficients.

    A note on interpretation. This is a small, cross-sectional sample, and the indicators are strongly correlated with one another — which is why some coefficients carry unexpected signs, as the original analysis also notes. The coefficients describe associations in these 39 countries, not causal effects, and estimates for countries far outside the observed ranges are extrapolations. Treat the output as an illustration of the modelling approach rather than an official hunger statistic.
